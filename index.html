Water Journey

  1. Greywater enters through the inlet pipe.
  2. Water passes through the filter container.
  3. Filtered water enters the solar still tray.
  4. Water evaporates and condenses on the cover.
  5. Recovered water flows into a collection bottle.

About the Project

The aim of ECOPULSE is to demonstrate water recovery and conservation using filtration, solar distillation and electronic monitoring.

Electronic Components

  • Arduino UNO v1888
  • DS18B20 Temperature Sensor
  • LCD Display

Safety: The recovered water is not certified as drinking water. Electronics must be kept away from water.
